Location and driving information

RadarPro uses location and speed derived from location services to calculate nearby radar, EDS and average-speed section alerts. Background protection requires the relevant location and background permissions. You can manage them in device settings.

When used, map and online routing services may send the map area, route origin/destination coordinates and technical request information to the service provider. Disabling location limits location-based alerts.

Information stored on your device

App preferences, permission and consent decisions, local Pro introduction progress, purchase-access cache and driving-section state may be stored on your device. These records support feature continuity and access decisions. Information you send in a support request is used to investigate your issue.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Payments are processed by the App Store or Google Play; RadarPro does not receive your payment card details. RevenueCat processes purchase/product information, transaction status and an app user identifier to verify purchases, manage Pro access and handle subscription lifecycle events.

RevenueCat privacy information

Optional advertising measurement

Meta advertising measurement is enabled only with your explicit consent; on iOS it also requires system tracking permission. When allowed, device/advertising identifiers, a Meta anonymous identifier and app activation information may be processed for measurement. Subscription and revenue events may be shared with Meta through RevenueCat. You can withdraw consent in the relevant app setting and manage iOS tracking permission in system settings. Advertising consent is not required to purchase Pro.

This website

The site stores your language preference in browser local storage. Google Fonts requests may send connection information to Google. Spoken examples use your browser’s speech engine; processing by the voice service depends on the browser and device provider. The hosting service may process IP addresses and technical connection information associated with page requests.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Preferences and introduction history are kept in app storage between launches until changed or the app’s local data is removed. On Android, the system’s Clear storage option removes local app data; clearing the cache alone does not. On iOS, Delete App removes the app and its related local data; offloading keeps documents and data. Device backups can retain a separate copy and may restore it after reinstallation. Deleting local data does not delete store transactions, third-party records or cancel a subscription.

The website’s saved language remains in your browser until you change it or clear this site’s stored data. Manage location, background and tracking permissions in device settings, and optional advertising measurement in the app.

For access, correction or deletion requests concerning information handled for RadarPro, write to support@radarpro.online. This includes support correspondence and purchase information processed through RevenueCat; deleting the app alone does not submit such a request. Records that must be retained for legal obligations are not necessarily eligible for deletion.

Providers’ separate retention and deletion practices are described by Apple, Google, RevenueCat and, where advertising measurement is enabled, Meta. Apple’s storage guide and Android’s storage guide explain the local device controls.
