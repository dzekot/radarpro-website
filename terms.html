While driving

RadarPro is a driving-awareness aid. Road signs, traffic rules and road conditions always take priority. Do not look at the screen or operate the app/purchase flow while driving. You are responsible for following local rules about radar assistance.

RadarPro and Pro

Within the same RadarPro app, Free includes the map and radar points, foreground alerts and manual database updates. Pro adds background alerts, automatic data updates/driving detection, advanced navigation and supported in-car features. Availability depends on device, permissions, connectivity, service configuration and vehicle compatibility. Free and Pro icons indicate access states of the same app, not a separate Lite app.

Initial Pro experience and store trial

An eligible new Free user may receive a one-time, device-local Pro-like introduction. Three distinct background radar/EDS alerts that actually finish speaking are the evaluation threshold; cancelled speech does not count. This introduction does not start a purchase, subscription or store trial and requires no card. The choice to continue is made in the app when not driving.

A separate 14-day store trial may be available with an eligible annual Pro offer. The App Store/Google Play purchase screen determines eligibility, the charge after the trial and the renewal date. A trial is not promised to every user.
